---
name: decide
description: Help make a decision: lay out the options, what matters, the trade-offs, and a recommendation.
---
1. Write the decision as one question and the deadline, if any.
2. List the real options, including doing nothing.
3. Ask what matters most to the owner and weigh the options against it.
4. Show a short table of each option's pros, cons and risks.
5. Recommend one, say why, and say what would make you pick another.
