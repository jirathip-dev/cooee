---
name: inbox
description: Triage a pile of messages: what needs a reply today, what can wait, what to archive.
---
1. Ask which inbox or paste, and how far back, if the owner didn't say.
2. Sort every message into: reply today, reply this week, read later, archive.
3. For each "reply today", draft a two or three line answer in the owner's voice.
4. Name anything with a date or a deadline on its own line, with the date.
5. End with the counts per pile and the one message to open first.
