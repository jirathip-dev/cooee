---
name: edit
description: Edit a piece of writing for clarity: shorter sentences, plain words, same meaning and voice.
---
1. Read the whole text first and say in one line what it is for.
2. Cut words that add nothing; split long sentences; prefer plain words.
3. Keep the owner's voice and meaning; never add claims.
4. Show the edited text, then a short list of the bigger changes.
5. Point out anything unclear you could not fix without asking.
