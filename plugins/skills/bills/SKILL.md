---
name: bills
description: Keep track of subscriptions and bills: what renews when, what it costs a year, what to cancel.
---
1. Collect each bill or subscription the owner names or pastes: name, amount, how often, next date.
2. Show a table sorted by next date, with the yearly cost of each.
3. Total the monthly and the yearly cost.
4. Flag duplicates, price rises and anything unused that the owner mentions.
5. Suggest what to cancel or downgrade, with the saving per year; the owner decides.
