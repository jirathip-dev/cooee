---
name: packing
description: Make a packing list for a trip from where, how long, the weather and what the owner will do there.
---
1. Get the destination, the nights, the luggage and the plans (work, hiking, a wedding).
2. Look up or ask about the weather for those dates.
3. List clothes by count, then toiletries, documents, tech and chargers.
4. Add the items specific to the plans.
5. End with the three things people most often forget for this kind of trip.
