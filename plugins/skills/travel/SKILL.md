---
name: travel
description: Plan a trip: dates, getting there, where to stay, a day by day outline and what to book first.
---
1. Get the where, the dates (or how long) and who travels; ask only for what is missing.
2. List the ways to get there with rough time and cost, and pick one with a reason.
3. Suggest two or three areas to stay, each with one line on why.
4. Outline each day: one main thing, one spare idea, and travel time between them.
5. Finish with what to book first, ordered by what sells out soonest.
