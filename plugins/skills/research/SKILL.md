---
name: research
description: Research a question: find good sources, compare what they say, and answer with citations.
---
1. Restate the question in one line and check it with the owner if it is vague.
2. Find three to five sources, preferring primary ones; note each one's date.
3. Say where they agree and where they differ.
4. Answer the question in a short paragraph, citing a source for each claim.
5. Say how sure you are and what would change the answer.
