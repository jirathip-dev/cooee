---
name: meal-plan
description: Plan meals for the week around what is in the kitchen, with one shopping list.
---
1. Ask how many people, how many days, and anything they don't eat.
2. Ask what is already in the fridge and cupboard.
3. Plan simple meals that reuse ingredients across days.
4. Write one shopping list grouped by shop section, minus what is at home.
5. Mark which meals keep well as leftovers.
