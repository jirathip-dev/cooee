---
name: weekly-review
description: Run a short weekly review: what got done, what slipped, and the three things that matter next week.
---
1. Ask what happened this week, or read the notes and chats the owner points to.
2. List what got done, then what slipped and why in a few words.
3. Ask what is coming up next week: meetings, deadlines, trips.
4. Propose the three things that matter most next week, each one sentence.
5. Offer to turn any of them into a routine or a reminder.
