---
name: meeting-notes
description: Turn a meeting transcript or rough notes into decisions, action items with owners, and open questions.
---
1. Read the transcript or notes once, end to end.
2. Write the decisions made, one line each.
3. List action items as: who, what, by when (say "no date" when none was given).
4. List the open questions nobody answered.
5. Keep it under a screen; quote the transcript only where wording matters.
