Side note

Side note: motion and touch (not drawn)

The large title collapses

The 34 px title scrolls with the list. Once it passes under the nav bar (41 px of scroll), the 17 px inline title fades in at the centre and the bar takes its blur and hairline, as in Chats · scrolled. Pulling down past the top stretches the title. A tap on the current tab, or on the status bar, scrolls back to the top. A second tap on the current tab goes back to that tab's root.

Swipe back

From the left edge (the first 24 px), a pushed screen (a chat, a persona's sheet in Settings, a round's lane chat) follows the finger. The screen under it slides in from 30 % with a fading dim. Letting go past a third of the width, or with a quick flick, goes back. Anything less springs back. ‹ Chats does the same. iOS's own edge gesture is not raced: the app's container sets overscroll-behavior-x: none. At 1440 there is nothing to swipe: the list and the detail are side by side.

Press states

A row, tab or chip tints (--press, the fg at 9 %) at touch-start, with no 300 ms wait (touch-action: manipulation, -webkit-tap-highlight-color: transparent). The tint fades over 150 ms on release, and a scroll cancels it. Buttons scale to 0.97. A long press on a row opens its menu: pin, mark read, archive. The tab bar and the rail answer on touch-start too.

Sheets

The persona sheet comes up as a page sheet with its grabber. Dragging it down dismisses it, and so does Cancel. On the 1440 window it is a centred panel, and Esc closes it.
