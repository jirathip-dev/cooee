# <Title>

## Problem
One paragraph: what is wrong or missing today, and for whom.

## Decisions
One line per answer the owner gave.

## Scope
Numbered list of what will be done.

## Out of scope
What will not be done.

## Done when
Checkable criteria, one per line.
