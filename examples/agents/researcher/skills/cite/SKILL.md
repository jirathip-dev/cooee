---
name: cite
description: How to cite sources in an answer: numbered links, quote dates, mark anything unverified.
---
1. Number each source in the order you first use it: `[1]`, `[2]`.
2. Put the list at the end: `[1] Title — site, date — URL`.
3. A claim with no source you could open is marked "(unverified)".
4. Quote a number exactly as the source gives it, with its date.
