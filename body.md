Closes #428

Slice 1 of the hot-files ADR (#356): `crates/cooee-server/src/lib.rs` is now generated by `scripts/gen.sh` (`just gen`). Its handlers sit in `routes/<area>.rs`, one area per row of `shapes.md`'s route table. `routes/mod.rs` is generated and chains every area's `routes(b)`. The wiring is in `serve.rs` (`router`, `serve`, `starter`, `runner`), the gate, timing and open paths are in `gate.rs`, and what handlers share is in `http.rs`. The root names are in `root.rs`, which is frozen.

## For humans
Nothing changes in the app or its API. Adding a server route now means adding one file under the server's routes folder and running `just gen`. Two PRs on different features stop colliding in the server's one big file.

## What changed
- `routes/`: threads, folders, rooms, logins, devices, pairing, push, report, personas, skills, plugins, mcp and routines. The pairing and routines handlers moved here from `pairing.rs` and `routines.rs`. `Pairing` and the routine `Runner` stay where they were, as the ADR says.
- Function bodies moved unchanged. The only differences are imports, `pub(crate)` on the shared items, and one `routes(b)` per area that registers the same routes as before. Registration now follows area name order; matchit matches by pattern, and the integration tests drive every route.
- `serve.rs` registers the routine runner with `app_context`, so routines exports `routes(b)` like every other area. Plugins registers its own `Dots`, the case `callers.md` describes.
- `root.rs` carries today's root `pub use` lines unchanged, plus `router` and `serve`, which used to be defined at the root.
